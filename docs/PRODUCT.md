# AI_Job_Scout

## Product Vision

AI_Job_Scout is an AI-powered job search assistant that helps job
seekers discover relevant jobs, understand job requirements,
prepare applications, find referral opportunities, and track
their job search.

## Target User

Job seekers who are actively looking for jobs and:

- Search across multiple job sources
- Have specific target roles
- Want jobs matching their skills and experience
- Search for jobs regularly
- Want to track their applications
- Want to find potential referral opportunities
- Want help preparing personalized applications

## Problems We Want to Solve

1. Job opportunities are spread across multiple sources.
2. Searching for jobs every day is repetitive.
3. It is difficult to identify which jobs are actually relevant.
4. Job seekers often lose track of applications and their status.
5. Tailoring resumes and cover letters takes time.
6. Finding potential referral connections is difficult.
7. Writing personalized referral messages takes time.

## Product Goals

AI_Job_Scout should help users:

1. Discover relevant jobs.
2. Analyze job descriptions.
3. Match jobs against their profile.
4. Understand skill gaps.
5. Prepare application materials.
6. Identify potential referral opportunities.
7. Prepare personalized outreach messages.
8. Track applications and outreach.
